## Bibliographic conventions

Publication years and author spelling generally follow the owner's CV. Some records use the year of first online publication, while volume/issue information refers to a later issue year (e.g., VIETHERB, Plant Metabolite Databases and iCYP-MFE). These are not duplicate publications. The Allium cepa record retains the CV's 2017 issue year; other indexes may display its 2018 online appearance. A reviewed Scholar BibTeX export can standardise dates and names across all records if desired.

Titles, authors, venues and DOI metadata were transcribed rather than abstracts or full research text. Research-topic keywords are website browsing categories. No citation counts or h-index figures are inferred.
